Physics

Q1 · Units & Dimensions
Planck's constant has the same dimensions as
Answer (3). h = E/ν has dimensions [ML²T⁻¹], the same as angular momentum (mvr).
Q2 · Kinematics
A ball is thrown vertically upward with a speed of 20 m s⁻¹. Taking g = 10 m s⁻², the maximum height reached is
Answer (2). H = u²/2g = 400/20 = 20 m.
Q3 · Kinematics
Two projectiles are launched with the same speed at angles 30° and 60° with the horizontal. The ratio of their horizontal ranges is
Answer (3). R = u² sin2θ/g; sin60° = sin120°, so the ranges are equal for complementary angles.
Q4 · Laws of Motion
A horizontal force of 20 N acts on a 5 kg block on a frictionless surface. The acceleration of the block is
Answer (2). a = F/m = 20/5 = 4 m s⁻².
Q5 · Laws of Motion
A block rests on a rough inclined plane with coefficient of static friction 0.5. The block just begins to slide when the angle of inclination is
Answer (2). At the angle of repose, tanθ = μs = 0.5.
Q6 · Work, Energy & Power
A body of mass 2 kg has kinetic energy 100 J. Its linear momentum is
Answer (2). p = √(2mK) = √(2 × 2 × 100) = 20 kg m s⁻¹.
Q7 · Work, Energy & Power
The work done in stretching a spring of force constant 200 N m⁻¹ by 10 cm from its natural length is
Answer (3). W = ½kx² = ½ × 200 × (0.1)² = 1 J.
Q8 · Work, Energy & Power
A body moving with speed v collides elastically head-on with an identical body at rest. After the collision
Answer (3). In a 1-D elastic collision between equal masses, the velocities are exchanged.
Q9 · Rotational Motion
The moment of inertia of a solid sphere of mass M and radius R about a tangent to its surface is
Answer (4). Parallel axis theorem: (2/5)MR² + MR² = (7/5)MR².
Q10 · Rotational Motion
For a solid cylinder rolling without slipping, the fraction of its total kinetic energy that is rotational is
Answer (2). K_rot = ¼Mv², K_total = ¾Mv², so fraction = 1/3.
Q11 · Rotational Motion
A disc rotating at angular speed ω has a ring of equal moment of inertia gently placed coaxially on it. The final angular speed is
Answer (3). Angular momentum is conserved: Iω = 2I·ω′ ⇒ ω′ = ω/2.
Q12 · Gravitation
The escape speed from Earth is 11.2 km s⁻¹. For a planet of the same mean density but twice the radius of Earth, the escape speed is
Answer (2). v_e = √(2GM/R) ∝ R√ρ, so doubling R doubles v_e.
Q13 · Gravitation
For a satellite orbiting very close to the Earth's surface, the ratio of escape speed to orbital speed is
Answer (1). v_e = √(2gR), v_o = √(gR), ratio = √2.
Q14 · Mechanical Properties of Solids
A wire stretches by ΔL under a load. Another wire of the same material with double the length and double the radius, under the same load, stretches by
Answer (3). ΔL = FL/(AY); L doubles, A becomes 4 times, so ΔL′ = ΔL/2.
Q15 · Mechanical Properties of Fluids
If the radius of a small spherical drop falling through a viscous liquid is doubled, its terminal velocity becomes
Answer (2). v_t = 2r²(ρ − σ)g/9η ∝ r².
Q16 · Mechanical Properties of Fluids

Answer (4). Continuity: A₁v₁ = A₂v₂ ⇒ v₂ = 4 × 2 / 1 = 8 m s⁻¹.
Q17 · Mechanical Properties of Fluids
The excess pressure inside a soap bubble of radius r and surface tension T is
Answer (4). A soap bubble has two surfaces, so ΔP = 4T/r.
