Recurring question types

  • Hardy–Weinberg: "4% / 9% / 16% affected by a recessive disorder — find q, carriers (2pq)".
  • ABO blood group: A × B parents with an O child — explain with genotypes IAi × IBi.
  • pBR322 figure: label ori, rop, ampR, tetR; insertional inactivation; growth on antibiotic plates.
  • mRNA ↔ DNA strands: coding strand = mRNA with T for U; template is complementary and antiparallel.
  • Base composition: A = 30% ⇒ G = C = 20%.
  • Numbers: 250 MMCs → 1000 microspores, 1000 pollen; 200 primary oocytes → 200 ova + 600 polar bodies.
  • Morgan's Drosophila: white/yellow 1.3% recombinants; white/miniature 37.2%.
  • Linkage map from distances (P–Q 20, Q–R 12, P–R 8 ⇒ order P–R–Q).
  • Ophrys pseudocopulation, cleistogamy, outbreeding devices, artificial hybridisation steps.
  • Source & use: cyclosporin A (Trichoderma), statins (Monascus), streptokinase (Streptococcus), citric acid (Aspergillus niger).

Assertion–Reason traps

  • "Sertoli cells secrete testosterone" — false (Leydig cells do).
  • "Snapdragon pink F1 is due to codominance" — false (incomplete dominance).
  • "Potato and stem tendril are homologous" — true (both stems), but A–R pairing must be checked.
  • "Vaccines give passive immunity" — false (active).
  • "Biogas is produced aerobically" — false (anaerobic methanogens).
  • "Exons are removed during splicing" — false (introns).
  • "Human insulin has chains A, B and C" — false (C peptide is removed).
  • "Apomixis and parthenocarpy are the same" — false.
  • "Crossing over occurs in diakinesis" — false (pachytene).
