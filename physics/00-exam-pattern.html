Assertion–Reason traps (these statements are FALSE)

  • "Increasing intensity increases stopping potential."
  • "Lenz's law violates conservation of energy" / "induced emf supports the change."
  • "Nuclei with A > 100 are most stable" / "A ≈ 60 nuclei are least stable."
  • "Fringe width is independent of λ, D and d" / "β ∝ d."
  • "Diamagnetic substances have small positive susceptibility."
  • "Potential inside a charged shell is zero."
  • "Nuclear force is proportional to charge."
  • "Radio waves have higher frequency than infrared."
  • "A concave mirror always forms a real, inverted image."
  • "Total energy becomes more negative as n increases."
  • "Zero net flux means E = 0 everywhere on the surface."
Strategy: learn the 16 derivations above, practise the 10 families, read photoelectric and TIR conceptually for case studies, and memorise the trap statements.
