Questions that keep coming back

  • Indigo: Why did Gandhi agree to a 25% refund? How was self-reliance linked to Champaran?
  • The Last Lesson: Franz's change in attitude; why the villagers sat in class; "language is the key to their prison".
  • The Rattrap: "The world is a big rattrap"; why the peddler signed as Captain von Stahle; Edla's role.
  • Deep Water: how Douglas overcame his fear; YMCA pool incident.
  • Lost Spring: Saheb's new job as loss of freedom; Mukesh vs the bangle makers; vicious circle.
  • Going Places: Sophie's dreams vs Jansie's realism; Danny Casey fantasy.
  • The Enemy: Sadao's dilemma — doctor vs patriot; Hana; the General.
  • On the Face of It: Mr Lamb's garden as a symbol; Derry's transformation.
  • The Third Level: escape from modern anxieties; psychiatrist's view; Galesburg 1894.
  • The Tiger King: satire on power; the wooden tiger; diamond ring; hundredth tiger.
  • Journey to the End of the Earth: Antarctica as key to Earth's past/future; phytoplankton.
  • Memories of Childhood: Zitkala-Sa's shingled hair; Bama's realisation; forms of resistance.
  • Poems: the "familiar ache" and smile (Mother at 66); Earth teaching us (Keeping Quiet); bower and "thing of beauty"; roadside stand's plea; fluttering fingers and chivalric tigers.
New-style long answers: compare two texts (e.g. Sadao and Mr Lamb on compassion; Zitkala-Sa and Bama on resistance; M. Hamel and Gandhi on rights), diary entries in a character's voice, and imagined conversations.
