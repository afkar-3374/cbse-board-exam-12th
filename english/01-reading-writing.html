1. Reading comprehension

Q. How should I attempt the two reading passages?22 M

  1. Read the questions first, then the passage — underline lines that answer them.
  2. For inference questions, give one clear reason from the text, in your own words.
  3. Vocabulary / opposite meaning: replace the word in the sentence with each option and check the meaning.
  4. Complete the sentence: use the passage's idea, keep grammar consistent with the stem.
  5. Data passages: percentage-point change = new % − old % (e.g. 45 − 35 = 10 points), not a percentage of a percentage.
  6. Stick to the word limit (15–20 or 40 words). Don't copy whole sentences for "explain" questions.

2. Notice (4 marks · ~50 words)

Q. Draft a notice for the school notice board about an inter-school quiz.format + sample

PM SHRI KENDRIYA VIDYALAYA, KOCHI ← name of institution NOTICE 20 October 2026 ← date INTER-SCHOOL SCIENCE QUIZ ← heading (caps) The Science Club is organising an inter-school quiz on 5 November 2026 at 10 a.m. in the school auditorium. Teams of two from Classes XI–XII may register with the undersigned by 30 October. Exciting prizes await winners. ← what, when, where, who, how Ananya Nair Secretary, Science Club ← name + designation

Marks: format 1, content 2, expression 1. Put the notice in a box; use passive/impersonal style; no "Dear students".

3. Formal invitation & reply (4 marks)

Q. Formal invitation (third person) and reply — acceptance or regret.format + sample

FORMAL INVITATION (third person, no signature) The Principal, staff and students of Kendriya Vidyalaya, Ernakulam request the pleasure of your company at the Annual Science Exhibition to be inaugurated by Dr. Meera Thomas, ISRO on Friday, 14 November 2026, at 10:00 a.m. in the school auditorium. RSVP: Aarav Menon (Secretary), 98xxxxxx01
REPLY — ACCEPTANCE / REGRET (formal letter style) 24, Hill View Road, Kochi ← sender's address 22 October 2026 The Principal Kendriya Vidyalaya, Ernakulam Subject: Acceptance of invitation to the Science Exhibition Sir/Madam, I thank you for your kind invitation to inaugurate ... I am pleased to accept and will reach the venue by 9:45 a.m. ... (Regret: thank → regret → reason → good wishes for success) Yours faithfully, Dr. Anjali Rao

Invitation answers: what, who (chief guest), when, where, RSVP. Reply: refer to the invitation by name and date, then accept/decline with a reason.

4. Letter to the editor (5 marks · 120–150 words)

Q. Write a letter to the editor about a civic issue (e.g. stray dogs, water-logging, noise, e-waste).format + structure

45, Park View Apartments Bengaluru – 560 034 ← sender's address 20 October 2026 ← date The Editor The Times of India, Bengaluru ← receiver Subject: Rising menace of e-waste in residential areas Sir/Madam, Through the columns of your esteemed daily, I wish to draw the attention of the authorities to ... ¶1 introduce issue ...causes, facts, examples, effects ... ¶2 describe problem ...suggestions: collection centres, awareness ... ¶3 solutions I hope the concerned authorities will take prompt action. Yours faithfully, Kavya Nair

Marks: format 1, content 2, expression 2. Use "Yours faithfully"; keep a serious, formal tone; give 2–3 specific suggestions.

6. Article (5 marks · 120–150 words)

Q. Write an article for the school magazine, e.g. "Reclaiming Attention in the Digital Age".structure + sample opening

RECLAIMING ATTENTION IN THE DIGITAL AGE ← catchy title By Riya Menon, XII-A ← byline ¶1 Hook: a striking fact/question — "The average teenager checks the phone 90 times a day ..." ¶2 Causes / present situation — notifications, reels, FOMO ¶3 Effects — shorter attention, poor sleep, lower grades ¶4 Solutions — screen-free hours, reading habit, apps that limit use, parents and schools as role models ¶5 Conclusion — call to action / hopeful note

Use a heading and byline (format marks). Write in an engaging, argumentative tone; include at least one fact and 3–4 suggestions.

7. Report (5 marks · 120–150 words)

Q. Report an event organised in school (health camp, ISRO talk, tree plantation).structure

ISRO SCIENTISTS INSPIRE YOUNG MINDS ← heading By Devika Rao, Student Editor ← byline Kochi, 18 October 2026: ← place + date (newspaper report) ¶1 What, when, where, who organised (5 Ws) ¶2 Chief guest, inauguration, sequence of events ¶3 Highlights — talk, exhibition, Q&A, quotes ¶4 Response/impact, vote of thanks, conclusion

Write in the past tense and passive/third person ("A health camp was organised ..."). Include at least one quote.
